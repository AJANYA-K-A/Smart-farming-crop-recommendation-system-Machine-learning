📖 Comprehensive Agronomy Guide

Understanding soil chemistry, environmental thresholds, and precision nutrient management.

🌱 Nitrogen Management

Crucial for leafy growth. For low Nitrogen, apply Urea (46% N) in split doses: 50% at basal sowing, 25% at tillering, and 25% at panicle emergence.

🌿 Phosphorus Management

Crucial for root strength and early blooming. Apply Single Super Phosphate (SSP) or Diammonium Phosphate (DAP) during soil preparation.

🪵 Potassium Management

Crucial for stem strength and drought resistance. Apply Muriate of Potash (MOP) to prevent leaf scorching and weak stalks.

🧫 Soil pH Correction

For acidic soils (pH < 6.0), broadcast agricultural lime or dolomite. For alkaline soils (pH > 7.5), incorporate gypsum or elemental sulfur.
