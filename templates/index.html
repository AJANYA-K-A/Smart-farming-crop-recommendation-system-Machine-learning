📖 Soil & Climate Guide

0–200 NPK (kg/ha)
6.0–7.2 Ideal pH
22 Crops Supported
  • 🌿 Nitrogen (N): Promotes leafy growth and vegetative vigor. Deficiency causes pale yellow older leaves.
  • 🌱 Phosphorus (P): Stimulates root establishment, flower blooming, and seed development.
  • 🪵 Potassium (K): Regulates water balance, disease resistance, and stem rigidity.
  • 🌡️ Temperature & Climate: Dictates metabolic rates and photosaturation thresholds.
  • 🧫 Soil pH: Governs nutrient bioavailability. Below 6.0 is acidic; above 7.5 is alkaline.
